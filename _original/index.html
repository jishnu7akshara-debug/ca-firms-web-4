Frequently asked

CA or CS: who do I need?

A CA handles accounts, audit and tax. A CS handles company law and ROC. We provide both.

Which tax regime is better?

We compute both and recommend the cheaper one before filing.

When is GST registration mandatory?

When turnover crosses the threshold, for inter-state supply, or for e-commerce sellers. Thresholds vary, so confirm with us.

Can you handle tax notices?

Yes. We review, draft replies and represent you before authorities.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Encrypted storage, restricted access and NDAs on request.

Do you work online?

Yes. Everything can be done remotely across India, including for NRIs.
